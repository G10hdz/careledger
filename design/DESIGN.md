## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, welcoming, and empowering environment for public care economies and municipal social infrastructure. Rejecting cold administrative bureaucracy and speculative fintech tropes, the visual language balances civic authority with warm, tangible compassion.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Humanist Civic:** Grounded in high legibility, tactile clarity, and welcoming warmth. Clean editorial structure harmonizes with soft structural containers to make complex municipal services feel dependable, protective, and human.
- **Dignity & Accessibility First:** Crafted to serve diverse citizen demographics—from frontline care workers to seniors and users with lower digital literacy. Every visual boundary is distinct, actions are unambiguous, and cognitive load is minimized through deliberate pacing.
- **Warm Institutional Trust:** Replaces harsh institutional gray with calm slate blues, vitality-rich botanical emeralds, and sun-lit amber tones that suggest growth, safety, and mutual community support.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to meet WCAG AAA contrast guidelines across primary reading contexts while maintaining a soothing, community-focused presence.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#0D9488` Deep Teal / Civic Emerald):** Represents care vitality, verified credits, and mutual aid growth. Used for primary calls-to-action, success confirmations, active citizen statuses, and core balance metrics.
- **Secondary (`#1E3A8A` Civic Slate Azure):** Anchor of municipal authority, public institutional security, structural navigation, and foundational typography. Conveys cryptographic and administrative trust without clinical stiffness.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` Terracotta Amber):** Expresses human empathy, urgent care requests, pending vouchers, and community milestones. Used for attention items, human-to-human endorsements, and time-sensitive care pledges.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC` Warm Slate White):** The background canvas. Tinted with subtle thermal warmth (layering soft touches of slate, amber, and mint) to eliminate harsh white glare on outdoor mobile screens and bedside care environments.
- **Surface Elevation Nuances:**
  - Background Base: `#F8FAFC`
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Subtle Highlight Surfaces: `#F0FDFA` (mint tint) and `#FFFBEB` (amber warmth)
  - Text Primary: `#0F172A` (deep slate for peak legibility)
  - Text Secondary: `#475569` (mid slate for guidance copy)
  - Border Subtle: `#E2E8F0`

## Typography

Typography prioritizes hyper-legibility, open letter apertures, and generous x-height to serve citizens across all visual ability ranges.

### Font Pairing
- **Headlines & Interface Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm, inviting geometry with humanist curves that project approachable authority without sterile chill.
- **Reading & Form Text (`Atkinson Hyperlegible Next`):** Specialized typography designed specifically to disambiguate similar letterforms (such as `I`, `l`, `1`, `O`, `0`), safeguarding vulnerable users against input mistakes on municipal welfare applications and credit transfers.

### Formatting Rules
- Never use font sizes below `12px`.
- Maintain a minimum of `1.45` line-height ratio on all body paragraphs to prevent visual overcrowding.
- Balance vouchers and metric totals using semi-bold or bold numerals paired with descriptive plain-language metadata labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a flexible, mobile-first columnar rhythm designed for PWA reliability across entry-level smartphones, tablets used by social field workers, and desktop agency portals.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Touch zones span the full grid width or dual-action columns.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates split voucher/claim interfaces for field caseworkers.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxed at `1280px` canvas width, centered with `3rem` margins to avoid horizontal eye fatigue.

### Touch Target Standard
All actionable nodes (buttons, chips, form radios, toggle segments, navigation bars) require a strict minimum touch target of `48px x 48px` with at least `8px` of perimeter spacing buffer to prevent accidental taps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses subtle atmospheric grounding rather than deep dramatic drop-shadows. This preserves clean contrast and avoids visual confusion for high-contrast viewing scenarios.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Tinted soft canvas (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Shells):** Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a low-contrast structural boundary (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Gives clean spatial boundaries without heavy shadows.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Drawers):** Soft ambient shadow with a faint cool slate wash: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & PWA Sheets):** Grounded civic overlay with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`) and centered elevation: `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (Active Citizen QR Vouchers & Credit Tokens):** Crisp high-contrast white card, accented with a 2px micro-border in primary emerald (`#0D9488`), rendering offline scans immediate and glare-resistant.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary employs human-scaled curvature (`rounded-2xl` and `rounded-3xl` equivalent radii) to remove sharp institutional severity and create tactile approachability.

### Radii Scale
- **Micro Radii (`0.375rem` / `6px`):** Small inline badges, status dots, and fine indicators.
- **Base Components (`0.5rem` to `0.75rem` / `8px - 12px`):** Input text boxes, compact chips, and secondary notification strips.
- **Primary Interactive Targets (`1rem` / `16px`):** Main touch buttons, floating bottom sheets, and segmented control containers.
- **Cards & Civic Containers (`1.25rem` to `1.5rem` / `20px - 24px`):** Community ledger balance cards, QR verification vouchers, profile summaries, and welfare benefit blocks.
- **Pill Utility (`9999px`):** Step tags, category filter chips, and primary validation pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Civic Action:** Solid `#0D9488` with `#FFFFFF` text. Minimum height `48px` (desktop) and `52px` (mobile PWA). Rounded `16px` corners. Font weight `600`. Active state features a subtle inward inset scale (0.98) for immediate physical confirmation.
- **Secondary Public Action:** Solid high-contrast slate-blue `#1E3A8A` with `#FFFFFF` text for official submissions or cryptographic ledger commitments.
- **Tertiary Empathy Action:** Warm amber outline (`2px solid #D97706`) with `#92400E` text on a gentle `#FFFBEB` hover state, dedicated to community requests and volunteer claims.
- **Destructive/Revoke:** Warm terracotta-crimson tone (`#DC2626`) paired with clear confirmation iconography.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **State Selection:** `44px` minimum height touch target. Unselected state uses `#F1F5F9` background with `#475569` text.
- **Selected State:** Filled with `#CCFBF1` (soft mint) with `#115E59` bold label and a 1.5px `#0D9488` outline.
- **Category Icon:** Accompanied by a 20px accessible Lucide or SVG icon on the leading edge.

### Cards & Ledger Blocks
- **Balance & Hour-Bank Card:** Rich background pairing pure white with a gradient rim or soft emerald-50 base. Displays available care hours or civic credits in `32px` bold font, contextualized with clear plain-language descriptions ("Valid for childcare, transit, or grocery co-ops").
- **Citizen Service Cards:** High-contrast bordered white card (`#FFFFFF`), featuring standard `1.25rem` inner padding, separated category icon, beneficiary details, and unambiguous status indicators.

### QR Care Vouchers
- Pure white container with high-contrast, edge-padded QR code block (minimum size `180px x 180px`) optimized for low-end camera scanners in fluctuating lighting.
- Accompanying numeric code presented in `20px` bold monospace format with letter spacing for manual fallback verification.
- Includes a live countdown or dynamic security token watermark to prevent duplicate usage.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Minimum Height:** `52px` touch area.
- **Borders & Background:** 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border over `#FFFFFF` background.
- **Focus State:** 2px high-visibility `#0D9488` border with a 3px soft outer ring in `#99F6E4` (teal glow). Never rely on outline removal.
- **Field Labels:** Positioned permanently above inputs in `14px` bold slate (`#1E293B`) to avoid floating label confusion for older users.
- **Assistive Text & Validation:** Generous 14px size directly below the input with unambiguous color-coded icons (Checkmark in `#0D9488`, Warning in `#DC2626`).

### Badges & Empathetic Feedback Banners
- **Success / Validated:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, leading emerald verification checkmark icon.
- **Urgent Need / Care Alert:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, leading warm amber calendar or heart icon.
- **Official GovTech Verification:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E40AF`, leading municipal crest or shield icon.

### Offline & PWA Sync Indicator
- A persistent, calm indicator at the top or bottom of the screen signaling local cache durability: "CareLedger Offline Ready: Actions will automatically sync upon connection", styled in calming slate blue with an unobtrusive sync pulse.
